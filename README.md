### 🧰 Open source: build from zero

Most people who want to build digital products fail because they skip the thinking (product, brand and go-to-market) that should come before the first line of code. This ecosystem is a guide plus Claude Code skills for each of those steps.

| Repo | What it is |
|---|---|
| [build-from-zero](https://github.com/ohanatais/build-from-zero) | The end-to-end guide: from raw idea to shipped product |
| [product-discovery](https://github.com/ohanatais/product-discovery) | Opportunity mapping, market research, validation, personas, positioning, PRD |
| [brand-and-copy](https://github.com/ohanatais/brand-and-copy) | Brand identity, tone of voice, product copy |
| [build-with-claude](https://github.com/ohanatais/build-with-claude) | Claude Code for non-technical builders, with every decision explained |
| [go-to-market](https://github.com/ohanatais/go-to-market) | Launch strategy and getting your first users |

**Who it's for:** aspiring founders, non-technical builders and PMs who want to build their own thing and understand every decision along the way, not just ship fast and hope for the best.
